Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the HMS system

Who can access the HMS system?

Access is role-based. Admins, Doctors, and Nurses each have separate dashboards with permissions tailored to their responsibilities.

How do I register a new patient?

Log in as an Admin or Nurse, navigate to the Patient module, and fill in the patient registration form with required personal and medical details.

Can doctors view patient history?

Yes. Doctors can access full patient medical records including past visits, diagnoses, prescriptions, and lab results within their dashboard.

How are appointments managed?

Staff can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments through the Scheduling module. Doctors receive notifications for upcoming visits.

Is this system connected to a real database?

This is a simulation environment. Data is stored locally for demonstration purposes and does not connect to any live hospital infrastructure.

What reports can be generated?

Admins can generate reports on patient statistics, appointment volumes, department workloads, and staff performance through the Reports module.
